# Returns Policy

Customers have **30 days from the delivery date** to start a return on a complete e-bike or accessory. The clock starts the day the carrier marks the order delivered, not the order date.

## What we will take back

- The bike must have **fewer than 50 km** on the odometer.
- It must be in resaleable condition: no crashes, no frame scratches beyond normal unboxing, no aftermarket parts.
- Accessories must be unused, with seals intact where they shipped sealed.
- Original packaging is preferred. If the rider discarded the box, CX can order a return kit for **$35**, deducted from the refund.

## What we will not take back

- Custom geometry or custom paint builds.
- Helmets, base layers, and opened nutrition.
- Bikes used as rentals, couriers, or other commercial work.
- Items returned after day 30, unless a manager issues a written exception.

## Who pays return shipping

The **customer pays return shipping** on change-of-mind returns. VoltPath pays return shipping when the item is dead on arrival, has a documented defect, or was sent in error.

Change-of-mind return labels for complete bikes are a **flat $89** in Canada. Accessory return labels are **$12**.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the **original payment method** in **5–8 business days** after the warehouse inspects the item. We do not refund to a different card.

If the rider prefers store credit instead of a card refund, add **10% extra store credit**. Store credit never expires.

## How to start a return

1. Open the order in Shopify and confirm the delivery date is inside 30 days.
2. Create a return in Zendesk using the "Return — bike" or "Return — accessory" macro.
3. Fulfilment email the label within one business day.
4. Inspection happens in Mississauga within two business days of receipt.

Do not promise a refund before inspection. If inspection finds more than 50 km or damage, we offer a partial refund or a send-back at the rider's cost.
