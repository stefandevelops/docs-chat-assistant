# Battery care

Share these points on every new-bike ticket and on any range complaint before you open a warranty claim.

## Daily use

- For daily commuting, charge to **about 80%** rather than 100%. Full charges are fine before a long ride.
- Avoid parking the bike in a closed car in summer. Packs above **45°C** should rest in the shade before charging.
- Do not charge below **0°C**. Bring the pack indoors and wait 30 minutes.

## Storage

If the bike will sit for more than two weeks, leave the battery at **30–70%** and check it monthly. A pack stored at 0% for a month can fall into deep discharge. Deep discharge is not covered by warranty.

Store the pack indoors. The garage is fine above freezing. Do not leave it on a concrete floor in an unheated shed over winter.

## Charging

Use only the VoltPath charger that shipped with the bike, or a replacement bought from us. Third-party chargers void the battery warranty.

A typical 500 Wh charge from 20% to 80% takes **about 2.5 hours**. 630 Wh and 720 Wh packs take longer. The LED on the charger turns green at full; it is normal for the fan to run.

## Range complaints

Range is not a fixed number. Wind, tire pressure, rider weight, temperature, and assist level all change it. Before you open a claim:

1. Confirm tire pressure is in the sidewall range.
2. Ask for the assist level they ride (Eco / Tour / Sport).
3. Ask for temperature. Below **5°C**, expect **15–25% less range**.
4. Ask for a display screenshot of cycle count and last full charge.

A capacity claim is valid only when Aftersales sees **less than 70%** of original capacity inside the 2-year or 600-cycle window. See the Warranty page.
