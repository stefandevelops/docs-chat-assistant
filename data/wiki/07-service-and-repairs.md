# Service and repairs

## Free first tune-up

Every new bike includes a **free first tune-up within 90 days of delivery** at a flagship store. It covers brake bedding, gear index, torque check, and a firmware glance. It does not include crash damage or worn pads from heavy use.

Riders more than 75 km from a flagship can use a partner shop. We reimburse **up to $70** for that first tune-up against an invoice.

## Flagship service counters

- Toronto — Queen West. Open Tue–Sat, 10:00–18:00.
- Vancouver — Main Street. Open Tue–Sat, 10:00–18:00.
- Montreal — Saint-Laurent. Open Wed–Sun, 11:00–19:00.

Bookings are required for service. Walk-in is for parts pickup and click & collect only.

## Mobile tech (GTA only)

A mobile technician covers the City of Toronto, Mississauga, and southern York Region, Tuesday to Friday. The call-out fee is **$95**, waived on warranty motor and battery jobs. Book at least **three business days** ahead.

## Turnaround

- Adjustment-only visits: same day if booked before noon.
- Warranty motor or battery swaps: **3–7 business days** depending on parts.
- Frame replacement: Aftersales quotes per case. Do not guess a date.

## Independent shops

Riders may use any qualified e-bike shop. We reimburse warranty labour up to **$120** with an invoice (see Warranty). We do not pre-pay shops. The rider pays the shop and we refund them.

Do not recommend a shop you have not checked on the partner list in Rideline. If the list is empty for that city, say so and offer the reimbursement cap.
