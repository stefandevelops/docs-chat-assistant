# Click & collect

Click & collect is available at the three flagship stores when Shopify shows local stock.

## Ready time

In-stock accessories are ready in **2 hours** during store open hours. Complete bikes are ready the **next business day** so the floor team can assemble and check torque.

If the store is already closed, the 2-hour clock starts at opening the next business day.

## Hold window

We hold a click & collect order for **5 calendar days** after the "ready" email. On day 6 the order is cancelled and the bike goes back to the floor. The payment hold is released in 5–8 business days, same as a refund.

## Pickup rules

- Photo ID that matches the order name is required.
- A friend may pick up with a photo of the order email plus their own ID. Add a note on the order.
- We do not hand a complete bike to a courier the rider sent unless the rider is on a recorded call with the store.

## Assembly

Flagship pickup includes the standard 85% assembly and a safety check. Full assembly (pedals, accessories, child seat) is **free on click & collect bikes**. It is not free on shipped bikes unless the rider paid the $189 in-home assembly add-on.

Child seats still need a second adult at pickup so the store can show the mount.
