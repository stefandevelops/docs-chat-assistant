# Trade-in

VoltPath takes trade-ins of our own bikes and of a short list of other brands ( Specialized Como, Trek Allant, Ride1Up, Aventon). We do not take scooters, gas bikes, or damaged frames.

## How a quote works

1. The rider submits photos and the serial through the trade-in form.
2. CX or Aftersales sends a quote within **two business days**.
3. The quote is valid for **14 days**.
4. The rider ships the bike to Mississauga with our prepaid label, or drops it at a flagship.

## Inspection

The warehouse inspects within **three business days** of arrival. If the condition is worse than the photos, we may reduce the quote by **up to 20%**. The rider can accept the new number or pay return shipping (**$89**) to get the bike back.

We do not pay out a quote before inspection.

## How the rider gets paid

Trade-in value is applied as **store credit** or as a **checkout discount** on a new VoltPath bike. We do not send Interac e-Transfers for trade-ins.

Store credit from a trade-in never expires and can be combined with a current promo unless the promo page says otherwise.

## What kills a quote

- Recalled batteries that we cannot legally resell.
- Missing serial or a serial reported stolen. Stolen serials are refused and we notify the rider in writing.
- Motor tunes above the legal assist limit.

If a serial is flagged stolen, stop. Do not offer store credit. Send the case to Aftersales and a manager.
