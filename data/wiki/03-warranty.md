# Warranty

Every new VoltPath e-bike includes a limited warranty for the original owner. Register the bike in the rider account **within 14 days of delivery**. Unregistered bikes are still covered, but claims take longer because we have to match the serial by hand.

## Coverage

| Part | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Frame (not paint) | **5 years** |
| Motor and controller | **2 years** |
| Battery | **2 years or 600 charge cycles**, whichever comes first |

The battery warranty is **2 years or 600 charge cycles**, whichever comes first.
| Display, sensors, charger | **2 years** |
| Wear parts (tires, brake pads, chain, grip tape, saddles) | **90 days** against manufacturing defects |

## What voids the warranty

- Opening, modifying, or swapping the battery pack.
- Water damage beyond the bike's published IP rating (City Commute and Cargo Haul are IPX5; Trail Peak is IPX6).
- Commercial rental, courier, or food-delivery use.
- Crashes, frame modification, or motors tuned above the legal assist limit.
- Proof of purchase missing and serial not in our system.

## How a claim works

1. CX collects the serial, photos, and a short description.
2. Aftersales replies within **two business days** with approve, deny, or "send to shop".
3. Approved parts ship free in Canada. Labour at a flagship is covered for motor, battery, and frame claims. Labour at independent shops is reimbursed up to **$120** with an invoice.

We do not cover a courtesy bike during warranty work unless the rider bought the **Ride-On Protection** add-on ($8/month). Do not promise a loaner.

## Battery capacity

A battery that holds **less than 70% of original capacity** inside the 2-year / 600-cycle window is a valid claim. Aftersales confirms this with a diagnostic export from the display, not with a guess from CX.
