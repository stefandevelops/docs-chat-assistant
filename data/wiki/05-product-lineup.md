# Product lineup

VoltPath sells four current e-bike families. CX should confirm the model before quoting range or warranty.

## City Commute 2.0

- Intended use: paved paths and city streets.
- Hub motor, 250 W nominal, 45 Nm.
- Battery: 500 Wh, claimed range **40–70 km** depending on assist and rider weight.
- Weight: 22 kg. Step-through and step-over frames.
- IP rating: IPX5.
- Price from **$2,195**.

## Trail Peak

- Intended use: gravel and light trail. Not a downhill bike.
- Fast mid-motor, 250 W nominal, 85 Nm.
- Battery: 630 Wh, claimed range **50–90 km**.
- Weight: 24 kg. Front suspension, 29" wheels.
- IP rating: IPX6.
- Price from **$3,495**. Free shipping applies because the price is over $2,500.

## Cargo Haul

- Intended use: kids, groceries, and cargo up to **80 kg** on the front rack plus rider.
- Rear hub, 250 W nominal, 65 Nm.
- Battery: 720 Wh, claimed range **45–80 km**.
- Weight: 32 kg. Dual-leg kickstand is required for loading kids.
- IP rating: IPX5.
- Price from **$3,995**. Child seats are sold separately and are **not** included.

## Ridge 20 (youth)

- Intended use: riders roughly **135–160 cm**.
- 20" wheels, 250 W, 20 km/h assist cap.
- Battery: 300 Wh, claimed range **25–40 km**.
- Price from **$1,495**. Youth bikes follow the same warranty table, but we do not offer Ride-On Protection.

## What we do not sell

We do not sell gas bikes, scooters, or motorcycle-class speed pedelecs. If a shopper asks for an unlocked 45 km/h mode, refuse. Canadian assist stays at **32 km/h**.
