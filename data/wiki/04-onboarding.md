# New-hire onboarding (CX)

This page is for customer experience hires in their first three weeks. Read it before you take a live queue.

## Week-by-week

- **Week 1.** Shadow two experienced agents. No live tickets. Finish the product walkaround and the returns/shipping modules.
- **Week 2.** Take email and chat with a buddy reviewing every send. Phone is still observed only.
- **Week 3.** Solo email/chat. Phone with a buddy on the line. You are cleared when your buddy signs the scorecard.

## Tools

| Tool | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Zendesk | All customer tickets. Never work a case only in Slack. |
| Shopify | Orders, refunds, addresses, serials on the notes attribute. |
| Rideline | Internal knowledge and macros. This wiki is the source of truth. |
| Slack `#cx-floor` | Quick questions. Not a case record. |

## Service levels

- First response on email and chat: **4 business hours**.
- First response on phone voicemail: **1 business day**.
- Public holidays follow the Holiday Hours page.

## Tone

Be warm and concise. Use the rider's name once. Do not joke about safety, brakes, or batteries. Never invent a policy number, a refund, or a delivery date. If the wiki does not cover it, say you will check and escalate.

## When to escalate in week 1–3

New hires escalate after **two failed resolution attempts**, or immediately when the ask is **$200 or more** in goodwill, a legal threat, a crash injury, or a press contact. Use the Escalations page. Do not CC your personal Gmail.

## Scorecard

Buddies score five live tickets on accuracy, citation of policy, tone, and time-to-first-response. You need **80%** or above to leave buddy mode.
