# Holiday hours

Support and stores follow this calendar. Warehouse cut-off still applies: orders placed after **14:00 Eastern** ship the next warehouse day.

## Closed (no support, no stores, no warehouse)

- 25 December
- 26 December (Boxing Day — warehouse closed; sitewide promo is online only)
- 1 January
- Good Friday
- Canada Day (1 July)
- Labour Day
- Thanksgiving (second Monday of October)

## Reduced hours

- 24 December and 31 December: stores 10:00–15:00, CX chat until 15:00 Eastern, no phone.
- Civic holiday (Ontario): Toronto store closed; Vancouver and Montreal follow their province.

## SLA on holidays

The 4-hour first-response clock **does not run** on closed days. It starts at 09:00 Eastern the next open weekday.

If a rider asks whether a bike will arrive before Christmas, only promise it when Shopify shows a delivery estimate on or before **23 December**. After 14 December we stop guaranteeing pre-Christmas delivery for complete bikes.

## Peak season

From **1 November to 23 December**, click & collect bikes may take **two business days** to be ready instead of one. Mention that in the first reply.
