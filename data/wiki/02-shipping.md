# Shipping

VoltPath ships complete bikes from the Mississauga warehouse. Accessories can ship from Mississauga or from a flagship store if that store has stock.

## Rates

- Complete bikes: **flat $79** shipping in Canada.
- Shipping is **free on bikes over $2,500**.
- Accessories: **$9.50** standard, or **free over $150**.
- White Glove delivery (room of choice, box removal, no assembly): **$149**.
- In-home assembly add-on: **$189** and only available in the GTA, Metro Vancouver, and Greater Montreal.

## Delivery windows

- Ontario and Quebec: **2–4 business days**.
- Rest of Canada: **4–8 business days**.
- Selected US states (WA, NY, MA, VT, CA): **6–12 business days**. We do not ship complete bikes to other US states.
- We do not ship complete bikes to **PO boxes or military APOs**. Accessories may go to a PO box.

A signature is required for every complete bike. The carrier will not leave a bike unattended.

## What to tell the customer

Give the window, not a calendar date, unless Shopify already shows a booked slot. Weather and border delays are outside our control; we do not compensate for a late window unless the order is more than **five business days** past the end of the quoted window.

Lost-in-transit claims open on business day 8 in Ontario/Quebec and day 14 elsewhere. File those with Aftersales, not CX.

## Packaging

Bikes ship 85% assembled. Pedals, handlebars, and front wheels are usually in the box. The rider (or a shop) needs hex keys and a torque wrench. We include a one-page setup card. The first in-store safety check is free at a flagship within **30 days of delivery**.
