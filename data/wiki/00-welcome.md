# VoltPath Help Centre

Welcome to the internal help centre for **VoltPath**, a fictional Canadian e-bike retailer. This wiki is sample data for a portfolio demo. It is not a real company and contains no customer records.

Use these pages when a shopper or rider asks about policy. If a page does not say it, do not invent it. Escalate instead.

## How to use this wiki

1. Search for the policy name (returns, shipping, warranty, trade-in).
2. Quote the written rule, including numbers and time windows.
3. If two pages seem to conflict, follow the more specific page and flag the clash in `#wiki-fixes`.
