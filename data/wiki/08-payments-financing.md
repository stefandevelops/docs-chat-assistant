# Payments and financing

## Cards and Interac

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Interac. Shopify is the only checkout. Do not take card numbers in chat or email.

Complete bikes over **$3,000** cannot be paid with PayPal. Accessories and lower-priced bikes can.

## Financing

Consumer financing is through **Affirm**. Terms are **3, 6, or 12 months**. Affirm decides approval; VoltPath cannot override a decline.

Minimum financed amount is **$400**. Youth Ridge 20 bikes can be financed if the applicant is 18 or older. We do not finance for a minor.

Promo "0% for 6 months" runs from time to time. If a banner is not on voltpath.example, the promo is not live. Do not honour a screenshot of last year's rate.

## Taxes and invoices

We collect GST/HST based on the **ship-to address**, not the billing address. Quebec orders also collect QST.

Corporate net-30 purchase orders need a credit application on file. Finance turns those around in **three business days**. Until then, the order stays unpaid.

## Failed payments and holds

If Affirm or a card fails after the order is created, we hold the bike for **48 hours**, then cancel and restock. Tell the shopper that in the first reply.

Chargebacks go to `finance@voltpath.example`. CX does not fight a chargeback in the ticket. Add a note and move on.
