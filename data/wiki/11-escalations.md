# Escalations

Use this page when a ticket leaves the normal CX lane.

## When to escalate

- Two failed resolution attempts on the same ask.
- Goodwill above the agent's cap.
- Legal language, a lawyer, or a threat to sue.
- Injury, crash, or battery fire.
- Press, influencer, or elected-official contact.
- A stolen-serial trade-in.

## Goodwill caps

| Role | Goodwill you may issue without approval |
| --- | --- |
| CX agent | **$50** store credit or a free accessory under $50 |
| L2 | **$200** store credit or a paid return label |
| CX Manager | **$500** |
| Director | above $500, in writing |

Never put goodwill on a personal card and expense it.

## How to escalate

1. Keep the Zendesk ticket as the record. Slack is not the record.
2. Post a short summary in Slack `#cs-escalations` with the ticket number.
3. Tag L2. If it is injury, fire, or press, also tag the CX Manager **Priya Chen**.
4. Stay on the ticket until L2 accepts it. Do not tell the rider "I have closed this."

L2 first response target is **4 business hours** on weekdays.

## Chargebacks and legal

Chargebacks go to `finance@voltpath.example` with the ticket number. CX does not write the representment.

Anything that looks like a legal demand goes to the CX Manager the same hour, even after the queue is closed. Do not offer a written admission of fault.
